About Us

Know More

Founded in 1997 by CK Toh, Toh’s Sign leverages decades of experience to efficiently resolve licensing challenges, ensuring smooth business operations.

Its subsidiary, Toh’s Sign Consultancy, specializes in complex licensing matters — including engineering/building plan approvals, Ministry of Health (KKM), Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture (MOTAC), Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA), and various industry certifications — providing tailor-made solutions for every client.
