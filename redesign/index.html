    About Toh’s Sign

    Founded in 1997 by CK Toh, Toh’s Sign draws on decades of experience to resolve licensing problems quickly, so businesses keep operating smoothly.

    Our subsidiary, Toh’s Sign Consultancy Sdn Bhd, handles complex matters, including engineering and building plan approvals, the Ministry of Health (KKM), the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture (MOTAC), the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) and industry certifications, with a solution tailored to every client.

    We believe exceptional teams create extraordinary results. We develop and keep our people through expertise, collaboration and an inclusive, supportive workplace.

    Vision
    To be Malaysia’s leading one-stop licensing consultancy.
    Mission
    One-stop solutions from consultation to license delivery, with responsive, customer-focused, high-quality service.
    Promise
    We commit only to what we can deliver: direct, fast, reliable and hassle-free.
    Social responsibility
    We help businesses operate compliantly, care for our community and invest in our people, with integrity at every step.
