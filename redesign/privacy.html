Privacy policy

How Toh’s Sign handles your personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). Last updated 12 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Toh’s Sign Informatic Sdn Bhd (713854-H) and Toh’s Sign Consultancy Sdn Bhd (1563660-H), B-2-5, Centre Point Medan, Jln 3/144a, Taman Connaught, 56000 Cheras, Kuala Lumpur.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email: your name, company, phone number, email address and message.
  • Information and documents you provide so we can prepare and submit your license applications.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and advise you.
  • To prepare, submit and follow up your applications with the relevant authorities.
  • To keep you updated on your case and remind you about renewals.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Who we share it with

We share your data only as needed to do the work you ask for: with the government agencies and local authorities that handle your application, with professionals engaged for your case (such as engineers or architects) where an application requires them, and and with the provider that hosts this website. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as we need it for the purposes above, and for as long as the law requires us to keep records.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw your consent, or limit how we use it. Contact us using the details below.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics, and does not track your visit.
