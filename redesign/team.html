Founder

Why Toh’s Sign exists

Founded in 1997 by CK Toh, Toh’s Sign draws on decades of experience to resolve licensing problems quickly, so businesses keep operating smoothly.

Since 1997

Over the years the work grew into two companies. Toh’s Sign Informatic handles business, advertisement and entertainment licensing. Its subsidiary, Toh’s Sign Consultancy, takes on the complex matters: engineering and building plan approvals, KKM, MOTAC, MIDA and industry certifications.

The idea has stayed the same: one team that prepares, submits and tracks your application through to approval, with a solution tailored to every client.
